# Discovery questions

Ask in this order. Write down who answered and mark guesses as open questions.

## Process today

1. What triggers the work (email, chat, form, ticket, schedule)?
2. Walk through the last three real cases end to end. Which systems did you open?
3. Which decisions need judgement, and what rule do experienced people use?
4. What are the most common requests (top 5 by volume)? Can you export a sample?
5. What goes wrong today (rework, waiting, escalations, complaints)?

## Volume and timing

6. How many requests per day / week? Peaks (hour of day, season, campaigns)?
7. Expected response time today and the target?
8. Which languages and channels?

## Systems and data

9. Which systems hold the answers (CRM, order system, helpdesk, docs, spreadsheets)?
10. Is there an API? Who owns it? Rate limits, test environment, credentials process?
11. Which data is personal or sensitive? Where may it be stored and for how long?
12. Where is the knowledge (policies, FAQs, product data)? How current, who maintains it?

## Risk and boundaries

13. What must the agent never do or say (promises, prices, legal, medical, competitor talk)?
14. Which actions need human approval? Any money, contract or account changes?
15. Who takes over when the agent cannot help, and in which hours?
16. Regulations or internal policies that apply (consumer law, data protection, sector rules)?

## Success

17. How will you know in 8 weeks that it worked? Which numbers exist today (baseline)?
18. What would make you switch it off?
19. Who signs off the spec, the pilot and full launch?

## Red flags to call out

- "It should handle everything" - no out-of-scope list yet.
- No baseline numbers - KPIs cannot be proven; propose measuring for 2 weeks first.
- Answers depend on undocumented tribal knowledge - knowledge work comes before the agent.
- No owner for handover - the agent will strand users.
